The Problem

Subsurface decisions need more than scattered logs.

Well logs give high-resolution rock and fluid measurements, but they exist only where wells are drilled. Seismic data covers the field more widely, but it does not directly measure reservoir properties.

Our project reduces that gap by learning the relationship between seismic attributes and measured logs, then producing predicted curves with validation, uncertainty awareness, and exportable results.
